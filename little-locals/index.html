Questions

What businesses usually ask.

Which cities are available for 2027?

Celina is the first planned edition. We’re also exploring select DFW cities for 2027. Tell us which city you want to reach, and we’ll confirm its availability before booking.

Who are the books for?

Little Locals is for local families with children, with a particular focus on welcoming newcomers. Existing residents can enjoy the activities, discover businesses, and use the local offers too.

Where will the books be distributed?

Distribution is planned through home builders, real estate professionals, and local community partners. Participating locations and quantities will vary by city as each edition launches.

How many copies are planned?

Each city edition has a minimum initial print run of 2,500 copies. Additional copies may be printed based on demand. Print quantity refers to books produced, not a guaranteed number of individual readers or household deliveries.

How long does a sponsorship last?

Your placement is for the named city’s 2027 edition. It remains included in any additional printings of that edition during 2027 at no extra placement charge. Future annual editions are separate bookings.

Are additional printings guaranteed?

No. The minimum initial print run is 2,500 copies per city; additional printing depends on demand. If we print more copies of your city’s 2027 edition during 2027, your placement stays included.

Is design included?

Yes. We create the custom activity or placement layout using your business information, branding, and approved offer where applicable. You review and approve the finished artwork before printing.

Can more than one business from the same category participate?

Category exclusivity is available with selected sponsorships. The specific category and which placements it covers are confirmed before booking. Resource and coupon placements are not automatically exclusive.

How do coupon placements work?

You provide an offer your business will honor, along with participating locations, expiration date, and any restrictions. We design the placement for your approval. A separate coupon-section placement can also be added to another sponsorship, subject to availability and an additional charge.

When will the editions launch?

Launch schedules depend on sponsorship bookings and production readiness in each city. We’ll confirm the schedule and payment terms for your edition before you book.
